DRY CREEK DUEL
==============
Wait for the bell, then draw. Move early and you're dead.
Beat five gunslingers before your three bullets run out.

HOW TO PLAY
  Space, Enter or left click ... draw / continue
  Esc or P ..................... pause
  Arrow keys or mouse .......... use the menus
  M ............................ sound on / off
  F11 .......................... fullscreen

Press within 100 ms of the bell (or before it) and it's a false start.
Win duels to unlock new arenas. Your fastest draw is saved.

Your progress is saved in a file called .dry_creek_duel.json in your user folder.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the game
is from a small independent developer. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
