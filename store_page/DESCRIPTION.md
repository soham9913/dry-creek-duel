## Description  (paste into the "Description" box; add gameplay.gif where it says so)

**Wait for the bell. Then draw.**

Dry Creek Duel is a quick-draw showdown about one thing: nerve. Step out onto the street, stare down your opponent, and wait. When the bell rings and DRAW! flashes across the screen, you have a fraction of a second. Move before the bell and you're the one going into the ground.

[ insert gameplay.gif here ]

**Five gunslingers stand between you and the Marshal's star**, each faster than the last:

- **Dusty Pete** — 450 ms. A warm-up.
- **Calico Jane** — 350 ms. Don't blink.
- **Dead-Eye Dan** — 280 ms. Now it's real.
- **Black Bart** — 240 ms. Few walk away.
- **The Marshal** — 200 ms. Legendary.

You get three bullets for the whole run. Lose a duel, spend a bullet. Spend all three and it's Boot Hill.

**Features**

- Six hand-crafted duel arenas, from a blazing desert sunset to a midnight railway depot, a ghost town sandstorm, a canyon bridge and a rain-soaked street
- Unlock a new arena with every gunslinger you beat
- Muzzle flash, black-powder smoke, camera shake, and windblown dust
- Fully synthesized sound: the bell, the gunshots and every arena's ambience
- Your fastest draw and best bounty are saved, so come back and beat them
- Plays in your browser, or download it for Windows

**Controls**

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Draw / continue | Space, Enter or left click |
| Pause | Esc or P (or the pause button) |
| Menus | Arrow keys or mouse |
| Sound on / off | M |

A run takes a few minutes. Mastering it takes longer. How fast is your hand?
